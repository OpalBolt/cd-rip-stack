# Episode identity is the stated range + disc position; durations grade, never assign

`skive rip tv` assigned episodes by greedy absolute-duration matching of disc titles against TMDB runtimes, which mis-identifies episodes on PAL discs (~4–5% runtime offset) and on near-uniform-length seasons — Generation Kill disc 1 is the regression fixture, where all three true matches lost by seconds. We replace the matcher: the user states a contiguous episode range in one pre-filled prompt, eligible titles are assigned in disc order to that range, and a per-title ratio audit (disc ÷ TMDB, green ~0.92–1.04) only flags surprises. Durations grade assignments; they never make them — they do prefill the range prompt as the episode guess (ADR 0008), which the human confirms or overrides. Fix rows remain the escape hatch for weird discs.
