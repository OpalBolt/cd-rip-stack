# Stall guard: budget, skip, continue

A disc with an unreadable section sends cd-paranoia into retry hell — the 2026-11-13 Enigma rip sat at ETA 15h+ (and climbing) on a 4:48 track — so `rip music` kills cyanrip on a per-track wall-clock budget of `max(10 min, 3× the track's audio duration)` (durations from a TOC-only `cyanrip -J` pre-pass), skips the stalled track, and re-runs the disc without it: a trip is a disc problem, not a patience problem, and the operator repairs the hole by hand after wiping the disc. cyanrip's own ETA cannot be the trip signal (healthy tracks on this drive peak at 42–87 min ETA during warm-up and encode; skive measures wall clock itself), and because cyanrip buffers compressed audio in RAM until the disc finishes (ReplayGain), a killed pass stages nothing — the continuation re-rips the healthy prefix, which is the only correct option. Never a retry (cd-paranoia already retried; a human wipes the disc and re-rips the track by hand), never an Interrupt (the run continues), and never armed alongside `-Z`, which is slow by design.
